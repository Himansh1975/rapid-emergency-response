Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

Who we are

This website belongs to Rapid Emergency Response Plumbing, run by Ryan Aubrey, a sole trader based in Batley, West Yorkshire. Ryan is responsible for any personal details you share with the business. You can contact him on 07543 599184 or Plumber247@mail.com.

What we collect

This website does not store any personal details itself. When you call, send a WhatsApp message or email, Ryan receives what you choose to share. That is usually your name, phone number, address, and a description or photos of the plumbing problem.

The "Send Ryan the details" form does not send anything to us directly. It opens WhatsApp on your device with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

How we use it

  • To reply to you, give you a price and arrange a visit.
  • To carry out the job, invoice you and keep records for tax purposes.

The legal basis is that you have asked us to provide a service (contract), plus our legal duty to keep business records. We never sell your details or use them for marketing lists.

How long we keep it

Job and invoice records are kept for 6 years, as required by HMRC. Enquiries that do not turn into a job are deleted within 12 months.

Website statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies and does not identify you personally. This site does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you by contacting Ryan using the details above. If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
