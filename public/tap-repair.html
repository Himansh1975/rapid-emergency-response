Tap repair & replacement

Dripping, stiff or broken taps fixed, or a new tap supplied and fitted in your kitchen or bathroom. Ryan replaces washers, cartridges and valves, and fits kitchen mixers, basin taps, bath taps and outdoor taps.

What to do right now

  1. A dripping tap isn't an emergency, but don't overtighten it. That damages the washer or cartridge further.
  2. If it won't turn off, shut the isolation valve under the sink with a flat screwdriver, or turn off the stopcock.
  3. Send a photo of the tap on WhatsApp so Ryan can bring the right parts.

Common questions

Repair or replace?

If the tap body is sound, a new washer or cartridge usually fixes it for a fraction of the cost. If it's corroded or parts aren't available, Ryan will tell you and price a replacement.

Can you fit a tap I've bought?

Yes. Send a photo or the product link and Ryan will confirm it suits your sink and pipework.
