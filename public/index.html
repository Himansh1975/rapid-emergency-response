What we fix

Plumbing services

Emergency call-outs and everyday repairs for homes, landlords and small businesses. Clear prices agreed before any work starts.

Leaks & burst pipes

Fast response to stop the water and stop the damage, then a proper permanent repair.

  • Burst and frozen pipes
  • Leaks traced and fixed
  • Water through ceilings

Tap repair & replacement

Dripping, stiff or broken taps fixed, or a new tap supplied and fitted in your kitchen or bathroom.

  • Washers, cartridges and valves
  • Kitchen mixers and basin taps
  • Bath and outdoor taps

Toilet repair & replacement

Blocked, running or leaking toilets sorted, and old toilets swapped for new ones.

  • Blocked toilets cleared
  • Constant running, flush and fill valve repairs
  • Full toilet replacement

Sinks, wastes & appliances

Leaking sink pipework put right and new appliances plumbed in neatly.

  • Leaking waste traps and pipework
  • Washing machine and dishwasher hook-ups
  • New sinks plumbed in

General plumbing

The jobs that don't need a big project but still need doing right.

  • Stopcocks and isolation valves
  • New and rerouted pipework
  • Cistern and overflow faults
