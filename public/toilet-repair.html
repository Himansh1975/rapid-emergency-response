Toilet repair & replacement

A toilet that won't stop running wastes water and money. One that's blocked or leaking can't wait. Ryan repairs flush and fill valves, clears blockages and swaps out old toilets for new ones.

What to do right now

  1. If it's overflowing, lift the cistern lid and push the float up, or turn the isolation valve on the feed pipe a quarter turn.
  2. Don't keep flushing a blocked toilet.
  3. If water is leaking round the base, stop using it and put towels down. It can rot the floor underneath.

What Ryan fixes

  • Blocked toilets cleared
  • Constant running, slow fill and weak flush
  • Flush and fill valve repairs
  • Leaks from the cistern or around the base
  • Full toilet replacement

Common questions

Why does my toilet keep running?

Usually a worn flush valve seal or a fill valve that isn't shutting off. Both are quick repairs that don't need a new toilet.

Can you supply the new toilet?

Yes. Ryan can supply and fit one, or fit one you've already bought. Either way the price is agreed first.
