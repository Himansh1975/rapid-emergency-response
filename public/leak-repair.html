Leak & burst pipe repair

A leak that's left gets worse, and water damage costs far more than the repair. Ryan traces where the water is really coming from, stops it, and puts in a proper permanent fix.

What to do right now

  1. Turn off the stopcock if water is escaping and you can't stop it at an isolation valve.
  2. Look for an isolation valve on the pipe feeding the leaking tap, toilet or appliance. A quarter turn with a flat screwdriver shuts it off.
  3. Put a bucket under the drip and move anything valuable out of the way.
  4. Note when it started and what was running at the time. It helps track the leak down faster.

Common questions

Can you find a leak I can't see?

Usually, yes. Ryan works back from where the water shows up, checks the likely joints, valves and pipe runs, and explains what he's found before fixing it.

Will you need to lift floorboards?

Only if the leak is under the floor. Ryan will tell you before doing anything and agree the price first.

Can you deal with a frozen pipe?

Yes. Don't use a blowtorch or boiling water on it. Turn off the stopcock and call. Frozen pipes often split, and the leak only shows once they thaw.
