Emergency plumber

Water coming through the ceiling, a burst pipe, or a toilet overflowing? Call Ryan on 07543 599184. He's a City & Guilds qualified plumber based in Batley, covering Leeds, Morley, Drighlington, Pudsey and Calverley.

What to do right now

  1. Turn off your stopcock. It's usually under the kitchen sink, where the mains pipe comes in. Turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. If water is near lights or sockets, switch off the electrics at the consumer unit and don't touch wet switches.
  3. Open your cold taps to drain the system and catch drips with buckets and towels.
  4. Take a photo or short video and send it on WhatsApp, so Ryan knows what he's walking into.

Common questions

What counts as a plumbing emergency?

Anything where water is escaping and you can't stop it, water near electrics, a toilet that's blocked or overflowing when it's the only one in the house, or no water at all. If you're not sure, send a photo on WhatsApp and Ryan will tell you whether it's urgent.

How much does an emergency call-out cost?

Ryan agrees the price with you before any work starts, so there are no surprises. Call and describe the problem, or send a photo, and he'll give you a clear price.

Do you do boiler or gas work?

No. Ryan is a plumber, not a Gas Safe engineer, so boilers and gas appliances need a Gas Safe registered engineer.
